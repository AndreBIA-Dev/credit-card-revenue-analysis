# Credit Card Revenue Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes credit card revenue data to evaluate performance across card categories and identify revenue trends over time.

The objective is to understand which card segments generate the highest revenue and observe overall revenue growth patterns.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains transactional revenue information segmented by:

- Client ID
- Quarter and Year
- Card Category
- Revenue Target

Analysis focuses on aggregated revenue performance.

---

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Kaggle Notebooks

---

## Key Analysis

### 1. Revenue by Card Category
Aggregated total revenue by card type to determine highest-performing segments.

### 2. Revenue Trend Over Time
Visualized revenue evolution across time periods to detect growth patterns.

---

## Key Insights

- Revenue is concentrated in specific card categories.
- Certain periods demonstrate stronger revenue growth.
- Segment-based strategy could optimize profitability.
